❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Clear answers to your governance, privacy, and service questions.

Is JanSetu completely free to use?

Yes! JanSetu is a 100% free public utility platform. We do not charge any fee for accessing government links, opening bank accounts, checking scheme status, tracking e-FIRs, or using built-in tools.

Which bank account is best for receiving government DBT scheme money?

SBI YONO, Kotak 811, and India Post Payments Bank (IPPB) are all 100% zero balance accounts that support direct Aadhaar seeding for PM-KISAN, Subhadra Yojana, and scholarship DBT payments.

Is my personal data saved when I use JanSetu?

No, never. All actions happen directly inside your browser or on official portal links. Nothing is uploaded, transmitted, or saved to any external central database.

Can I register GST and file monthly returns without hiring a CA?

Yes. The Indian tax framework operates on a self-assessment model. Any business owner with an Aadhaar-linked mobile number can complete new GST registration (GST REG-01) and file GSTR-1 / GSTR-3B monthly returns directly using free Aadhaar OTP verification.
