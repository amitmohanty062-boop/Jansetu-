❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Clear answers to your governance, privacy, and service questions.

Is JanSetu completely free to use?

Yes! JanSetu is a 100% free public utility platform. We do not charge any fee for accessing government links, checking scheme status, tracking e-FIRs, or using built-in photo resizers and resume tools.

Is my personal data saved when I resize photos or generate a resume?

No, never. All document resizing, photo compression, and PDF generation happen 100% locally inside your browser memory. Nothing is uploaded, transmitted, or saved to any central database.

Can I register GST and file monthly returns without hiring a CA?

Yes. The Indian tax framework operates on a self-assessment model. Any business owner with an Aadhaar-linked mobile number can complete new GST registration (GST REG-01) and file GSTR-1 / GSTR-3B monthly returns directly using free Aadhaar OTP verification.

How do I track my filed CPGRAMS grievance or Subhadra Yojana payment?

Select the respective service card on JanSetu and click "Access Portal Directly". You will be routed to the official government dashboard where you can enter your Registration / Aadhaar / Application ID to check real-time status.
