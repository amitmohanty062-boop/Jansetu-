🔒 Privacy & how JanSetu works

This is a plain-language note, last updated 13 October 2026.

What stays on your phone

  • My Details, My Vault, Track Status, favourites, resume and RTI text are saved only in your browser on this phone. JanSetu has no accounts and no server that receives them. Clearing your browser data deletes them.
  • Photo resizer, Photos to PDF, resume and RTI tools work on your phone. Your files are not uploaded to JanSetu.

What uses other services

  • Voice search and read-aloud use your phone's or browser's speech service (for example Google). Their privacy terms apply to your voice.
  • The lawyer list is loaded from a public Google Sheet of advocates who chose to be listed.
  • WhatsApp, UPI apps and government portals open outside JanSetu. We do not control them. What you send on WhatsApp is seen by us and by WhatsApp.
  • Anonymous page-view counts are collected with a privacy-friendly counter. No names, phone numbers or saved items are included.

Please remember

  • JanSetu is not a government website. Always confirm details and fees on the official portal.
  • Never share passwords or OTPs, with anyone.
  • Tools give general information, not legal advice.
